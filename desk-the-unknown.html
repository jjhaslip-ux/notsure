The Desks

The Unknown

UAP, the unexplained, and the stories that arrive before the answers do. This desk reads the official record first — the reports, the case resolutions, and the documents — and treats everything else as a lead, not a conclusion.

What this desk covers

Government UAP disclosures and reports, official case resolutions, congressional hearings and the annual reports to Congress, and the paper trail as agencies release it. Sighting reports are signals to check, never stories in themselves — the same verification rule as every other desk: two independent sources, or one primary source, before anything runs as Confirmed.

Sources

These are the desk’s watch sources, read on their own pages; report drops on this beat are documents, not feeds.

  • AARO — All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office — the Pentagon’s UAP office: official reports and case resolutions (Page watch)
  • NASA — the agency’s UAP study, statements, and science (Page watch)
  • Office of the Director of National Intelligence — the annual UAP reports delivered to Congress (Page watch)
  • U.S. Department of Defense — official releases, transcripts, and published UAP imagery (Page watch)
  • National Archives — the federal UAP records collection, released by law (Page watch)
  • FAA — pilot reporting and airspace safety information (Page watch)
  • National UFO Reporting Center — public sighting reports, filed by the witnesses themselves (Signal only — a lead, never confirmation)

Stories from this desk run on the wire under the desk’s name. If you have a document this desk should see, it belongs on the record — bring it to the desk, and it will be read at the source.
