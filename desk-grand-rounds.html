The Desks

Grand Rounds

Healthcare across Florida, Georgia, and Alabama — the case presented in the open. This desk reads care at the source: the state health agencies, the federal health agencies, the licensing boards, and the newsrooms that cover medicine daily.

What this desk covers

Public health, hospitals and health systems, state health agencies, insurers, and the care workforce — the nurses, techs, and clinicians who do the work. Outbreaks and advisories, approvals and recalls, licensing and discipline, and the money that decides how care is paid for. If it is decided about care in the three states, it is presented here — from the record first, before anyone’s summary of it.
