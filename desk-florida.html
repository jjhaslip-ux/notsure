The Desks

Florida

Tallahassee and state government — the laws and money that move Florida. This desk reads the state at the source: the Governor’s releases, both chambers, the agencies, and the newsrooms that cover them daily.

What this desk covers

Session and interim committee work, the budget, agency rulemaking, elections administration, and the courts. If it is decided in Tallahassee, it lands here first — from the state’s own paper before anyone’s summary of it.

Sources

Status below is from the desk’s November 2026 source audit: a source marked verified feed publishes a working feed the desk reads directly; page watch sources are read on their own pages.

  • Florida Governor's Office — press releases from the Governor (Page watch)
  • Florida Senate — bills, daily calendars, and session video (Verified feed)
  • Florida House of Representatives — bills and committee work from the House (Page watch)
  • Florida Department of Health — state health advisories and releases (Verified feed)

  • News Service of Florida — the subscriber statehouse wire (Listed, unverified)

Stories from this desk run on the wire under the desk’s name. If you have a document this desk should see, it belongs on the record — bring it to the desk, and it will be read at the source.
