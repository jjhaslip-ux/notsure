About the Outlet

NS-FLUID

An independent news outlet, based in Florida — reporting world news.

NS-FLUID reports world news from Florida: the capitals and the coasts, the statehouses and the parliaments, the space economy lifting off every shore that launches, and the stories that arrive before the answers do. The home ground — the Florida Panhandle, the Gulf coast, and the three home states — is covered first and closest, from the desk's own chair.

The mark is a saline IV bag lettered NS-FLUID — the news, on a drip.

Saline is the first thing hung for nearly every patient: steady, measured, life-sustaining while the real work gets done. That is the job as this desk understands it. The outlet’s north star is unchanged — Agape, the fourth of the seven kinds of love: selfless, community-first. It is not decoration. Before a story goes up, the desk asks one question: does this serve the people here? If the answer is no, it drops down the queue. Everything the outlet publishes carries that mark and answers to it.

The desks. Florida, The Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Africa, Space, Grand Rounds, and The Unknown. Each desk opens with its charter and builds from there.

The files. The dossiers published here are open-source work: compiled from official records, published reporting, and the public history of the people in them. Sources are named in every file. No private data is accessed — patience and a search bar are the whole method.

How we source

Every story on NS-FLUID carries its sources with it. The rule: two independent sources, or one primary source, before a story is called confirmed — and every story is labeled confirmed, developing, or unverified so a reader can see exactly how sure we are. Claims are attributed to whoever made them. We summarize and link; we do not copy a source’s wording. When we get something wrong, the correction is public and dated, in the corrections log.

Standing alone. NS-FLUID is its own outlet, under its own masthead. What it publishes, it publishes under the badge.
