About the Outlet

NS-FLUID

An independent Florida news outlet, based in the Florida Panhandle.

NS-FLUID covers Florida: the statehouse in Tallahassee, the coast it sits on, the space economy lifting off its eastern shore, and the stories that arrive before the answers do.

The mark is a badge: a white four over emerald Gulf water, ringed in silver — the home coast engraved at the base.

The four is Agape — the fourth of the seven kinds of love: selfless, community-first. It is not decoration; it is this outlet’s editorial north star. Before a story goes up, the desk asks one question: does this serve the people here? If the answer is no, it drops down the queue. The water is home — the emerald green the Gulf actually runs off this coast, the water this coast is known for. Everything the outlet publishes carries that mark and answers to it.

The desks. Statehouse, Emerald Coast, Space Coast, and The Unknown. Each desk opens with its first stories and builds from there.

The files. The dossiers published here are open-source work: compiled from official records, published reporting, and the public history of the people in them. Sources are named in every file. No private data is accessed — patience and a search bar are the whole method.

How we source

Every story on NS-FLUID carries its sources with it. The rule: two independent sources, or one primary source, before a story is called confirmed — and every story is labeled confirmed, developing, or unverified so a reader can see exactly how sure we are. Claims are attributed to whoever made them. We summarize and link; we do not copy a source’s wording. When we get something wrong, the correction is public and dated, in the corrections log.

Standing alone. NS-FLUID is its own outlet, under its own masthead. What it publishes, it publishes under the badge.
