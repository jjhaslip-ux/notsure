Space Coast

Cape Canaveral and Brevard County — launches, the range, and the space economy. What leaves Florida for orbit starts here, and this desk reads it from the agencies that fly it.

What this desk covers

Launch schedules and range notices, NASA and Space Force operations at the Cape, the contractors and workforce of the coast, and what the space economy means for the towns underneath the flight path.
