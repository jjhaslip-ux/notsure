Georgia

Atlanta and statewide — the Gold Dome, the agencies, and the state around them. Georgia joined the masthead in November 2026, and this desk reads it the same way as home: primary paper first.

What this desk covers

The Governor’s office and the General Assembly, state agencies and courts, elections, and the Atlanta metro — with Georgia’s nonprofit statehouse wire and public broadcasting doing the daily lifting alongside the primary sources.
